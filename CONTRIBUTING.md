# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep this list honest — free hosting tiers change fast.

## What we accept

- **A tier that changed** — started sleeping, cut its limits, or died (link the provider's own docs/changelog).
- **A new platform** with a genuine free-forever tier.
- **Dead-link fixes.**

## Rules that keep this useful

1. **Source every claim** — link the provider's official pricing/terms, not a blog.
2. **Keep the `Sleeps?` and `Expires?` columns honest** — those are the whole point.
3. **Distinguish "free forever" from "free trial credit."** A trial that runs out is not free-forever; say so.
4. Keep `README.md` and `data/platforms.json` in sync when you edit a row.

## How

1. Fork, branch, edit.
2. Open a PR describing what changed and linking your source.

Small, sourced, honest PRs get merged fast.
